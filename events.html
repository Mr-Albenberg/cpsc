The year at CPSC

Three seasons of sending

01
Fall semester

Fall

There’s no better way to kick off a new school year than with a classic Ski Club rager. You can look forward to a party every weekend of Fall semester, courtesy of your Central Pacific Ski Club membership. Fall is a great way to get to know your fellow members and the exec board in typical ski club fashion — with the music blasting and a drink in hand. Expect DJs, kegs, and enough jungle juice to go around. From our WOW week activities to our legendary Halloween party, the Executive Board solemnly swears you will not get bored this Fall.

02
Winter break + spring semester

Winter

The heart and soul of our club lives in the Winter season. We host our annual Big Trip during the LAST week of winter break, starting the ski season off with a BANG. Picture the most fun ski trip you’ve ever been on, multiply the fun by 69 and make it a week long — that’s basically our Big Trip. After that, we have four small trips every other week throughout the spring semester. These trips are WILD, and we wouldn’t miss them for the world. Fair warning: if you are not prepared to leave the mountain with 50 new best friends, then you shouldn’t come.

03
Spring semester

Spring

Come spring, when the sun is shining and the birdies are chirping, our dayger season begins. Almost every weekend brings a new dayge, beach day, die tourney, and if the snow is good… SPRING SKIING! Spring is also home to our fabled houseboating trip — a weekend getting boozy and groovy with your fellow senders, and one of the coolest trips of the year. By the end of spring semester our official events come to a close, and we hope to see everyone again next school year!
