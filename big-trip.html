Red Mountain, BC — CPSC went international

For the very first time, CPSC went INTERNATIONAL. From December 13–20, 2025 we sent it up north for five full days of skiing in CANADA, with an entire hostel rented out walking distance from the mountain, hostel parties and nights out at local bars (where the drinking age is only 19). Our cheapest Big Trip ever, and easily the most unforgettable.

Round-trip bus transportation was included — the bus ride isn’t just a way to get there, it’s part of the Big Trip experience — with a Non-Bus option for anyone arranging their own ride.

Good to know

Big Trip policies

From the 2025 trip — check the sign-up page for this year’s exact terms.

Refunds

Because CPSC must cover large prepaid expenses to secure housing, transportation, and other logistics, refunds cannot be offered for any reason. If you need to cancel after reserving your spot, the only way to avoid losing your payment is if a waitlisted person takes your place or you find a replacement traveler. Otherwise, your spot and payment are forfeited.

Deposits & payment deadlines

In 2025, a $99 deposit secured a place at registration (or you could pay in full), with the $600 balance due by November 15th through Venmo or PayPal. Missing the deadline meant losing the spot and the deposit, and the deposit option closed October 15th.

Passports & border crossing

For international trips you must have a valid, non-expired passport by departure. If you are denied entry for any reason — including intoxication, missing documentation, criminal record, or possession of illegal substances — you are responsible for getting yourself home, without a refund. CPSC is not liable for lost or stolen passports or documents.

Rules, damages & safety

All attendees are expected to follow both club rules and house rules. You are personally responsible for any damage you cause and will pay in full for anything you break. CPSC isn’t responsible for your personal belongings. Illegal substances are strictly prohibited and can mean immediate removal from the trip without a refund. CPSC is not liable for accidents, injury, or illness during the trip, but medical treatment will be provided if necessary.
