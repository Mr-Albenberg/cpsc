Since 1941

Chasing powder & a ridiculously good time

Founded in 1941, the Central Pacific Ski Club (CPSC) has grown into the second-largest collegiate ski club in the nation! CPSC brings together over a thousand snow lovers who are all about chasing powder and having a ridiculously good time, on and off the mountain. Whether you’re here to send it on the slopes or attend the best parties, you’ll find your people with us.

Every year, we hit the road for 7–8 trips to some of the best mountains in the country — think Tahoe, Utah, Colorado, and more. Whether you’re all about first chair on a powder day or just in it for the après, there’s a spot for you on all our trips.

Our members

Who are we?

The heart and soul of our club are our members. No other club in the area has such a diverse membership base composed of so many amazing people with different backgrounds and unique talents. Ski Club has a legendary event calendar, and between fall parties, our annual BIG TRIP, small trip season, spring dayges, spring skiing and houseboating, we have something for everyone! No reason to be board when you can join Central Pacific Ski Club!

Everyone

Who can join?

Everyone! Seriously. CPSC welcomes all skill levels, from first-timers to seasoned pros, and even those who just want to come along for the good times, killer events, and community. The best part about CPSC is the diversity of our members. We accept anyone and everyone with an interest in snow sports regardless of skill level, or even interest in snow sports for that matter. We can’t wait to meet you!
