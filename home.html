Who we are

Your people.
Powder days.

We’re proud to be one of the largest social clubs in San Luis Obispo, where our goal is to build community through unforgettable trips, weekly events, and a shared passion for skiing and partying. CPSC is run by an executive board of 10 members, putting together the best events and membership experience.

Every winter break, we send a legendary week-long trip to some awesome resorts — past destinations include Jackson Hole, Steamboat, and Big Sky. During Winter (Spring Semester), we keep the stoke alive with smaller trips to places like Oregon, Tahoe, Mammoth, and Utah.

But the fun doesn’t stop when the snow melts. Spring means it’s time for our annual spring skiing trip and houseboating. As well as our awesome spring trips, we host weekly events throughout the year to keep everyone connected and excited about ski club. We take pride in being super inclusive — all levels are welcome, and we’re always welcoming and encouraging new members!

If you’re looking for your people, powder days, and your best college memories, you’ve found the right place. See you on the slopes (or the boat)!
